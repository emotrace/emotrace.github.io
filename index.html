EmoTrace Hierarchical Masked Diffusion Transformer
for Fine-Grained Temporal
Speech Emotion Recognition

Abstract

Speech emotion recognition is essential for understanding human communication and enabling natural human–machine interaction. However, coarse clip-level predictions overlook subtle emotional distinctions and changes over time. We introduce EmoTrace, a hierarchical masked diffusion Transformer that generates time-aligned coarse and fine emotion trajectories from speech. Masked reconstruction learns temporal and hierarchical dependencies, while training with model-generated context enables our method to iteratively revise uncertain predictions. To obtain supervision at scale, we construct EmoForge through automated consensus annotation of 1.9 million real and synthesized speech clips. During training, online concatenation converts available clip labels into weak temporal supervision without manual temporal annotation. We further curate EmoScope, a benchmark with human clip-level and temporal annotations across diverse speech sources and challenging conditions. On EmoScope, EmoTrace improves frame-level fine-grained accuracy by 18.9 percentage points over the strongest audio-language baseline. Evaluation on six external corpora from EmoBox further demonstrates robust and accurate emotion recognition across languages. These trajectories could support emotion-aware dialogue and fine-grained control of expressive speech generation.
